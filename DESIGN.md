# LifeFlow visual system

A person checks tomorrow's responsibilities on a phone at the kitchen table in daylight, then checks a reminder in a dim commute. Follow the device theme with a manual override.

Restrained indigo accents, ink text, pale slate surfaces and green completion states. Mobile tokens use the brief's #6366F1 primary, #8B5CF6 secondary, #22C55E success, #0B1020 dark and #F8FAFC light; web uses corresponding OKLCH colors. System typography with 32/24/18/16/13 sizes and strong label contrast. Rounded action buttons have at least 44px targets. Rows and section spacing establish hierarchy; cards group distinct actions. No decorative motion or sensitive push previews. Forms remain inline, with explicit pending and failure states.
