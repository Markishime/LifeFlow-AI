# LifeFlow AI

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals, students, professionals, families and freelancers in the Philippines managing everyday administration on Android and iOS. English is default with Filipino settings and user-selected timezone.

## Product Purpose

Organize Life. Automate the Important. Convert responsibilities and documents into reviewable tasks, persistent reminders and traceable workflows. Success means a user can create a task, receive a reminder and understand what actually happened.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, friendly. Respect the user's attention and explain uncertainty plainly.

## Anti-references

The brief rejects disconnected demonstration screens, chatbot-only experiences and visual decoration that impairs usability. Avoid invented obligations and false automation success.

## Design Principles

Make the next action obvious. Keep manual administration useful without AI. Require review before inferred actions. Show actual history and uncertainty. Request permissions at the point of use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible labels, scalable text, clear touch targets, loading/error/empty states, light and dark themes, reduced-motion awareness. English and Filipino localization architecture.
